The short version

Brightburst Arcade is built for children. We do not ask children for any personal information, we do not create accounts for them, and we do not send anything they do to a server. Game scores live in the browser on your own device.

Information we collect from children

None. There is no sign-up, no name, no email address, no age entry, no photo upload, no chat and no profile. A child can use every free game without providing anything at all.

Information stored on your device

The site writes a small amount of data to your browser's local storage:

  • Best scores for each game
  • A total star count and a count of rounds played
  • Whether sound is on or off
  • Whether this browser has a membership

This never leaves your device. It is not a cookie, it is not sent with any request, and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, as does the Erase saved scores button on the grown-ups page.

Advertising

This build reserves clearly labelled space for advertising but no ad network is connected, so no ad request or tracking script runs. If advertising is switched on in future, it will be limited to child-directed, contextual, non-personalised inventory: no behavioural profiling, no retargeting, no cross-site tracking, and no ad identifiers tied to a child. Members see no advertising space at all. This page will be updated before any network goes live.

Analytics

There is no analytics or tracking script on the child-facing pages of this build.

Third parties

Page fonts are requested from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which means your browser's IP address is visible to Google when a font loads. Nothing else is requested from a third party. If you would rather avoid this entirely, the fonts can be self-hosted — see the project README.

Payments

Payments are not enabled in this build. When they are, checkout will be handled by a third-party payment provider, reachable only through the parent gate, and card details will never touch this site. This page will name that provider before it goes live.

Parent gate

A multiplication question sits in front of payment and any off-site link. It exists to stop a young child wandering somewhere they shouldn't. It is not authentication and nothing sensitive relies on it.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected or stored on a server, there is no account to access, correct or delete. Everything the site knows is on your own device and under your control. If you have a question, write to [YOUR EMAIL].

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top will change and the new version will be posted here before the change takes effect.
