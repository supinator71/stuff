For grown-ups

The short version: five games, no accounts for kids, no chat, no behavioural advertising, and everything a child does stays in their own browser.

What we collect

From a child: nothing. High scores and star totals are written to the browser's own local storage on your device. They never leave it, and clearing your browser data erases them.

There is no account for kids, no email capture, no chat, no user-uploaded content and no third-party analytics in the child-facing pages.

Full detail in the privacy policy.

What kids can't do

  • Talk to anyone — there is no messaging of any kind.
  • Leave the site by accident — outbound and payment links sit behind a parent gate.
  • Spend money — checkout is only reachable through that gate.
  • See personalised ads — ad space is non-personalised only.

Questions grown-ups ask

What ages is this for?

Roughly 4–10. Counting Carnival scales its own difficulty as a child gets answers right, so it stays usable across that whole range.

Does it work on a school Chromebook or an old tablet?

Yes. The whole site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no build step. Once a page has loaded it will keep working without a connection.

Is there any advertising?

Ad space is reserved and clearly labelled, but no ad network is connected in this build — the slots show placeholders. If one is connected later it will be a child-directed, non-personalised network only, meaning no behavioural profiling and no retargeting. Members see no ad space at all.

What is the parent gate?

A multiplication question in front of payment and off-site links. It is a speed bump to keep a five-year-old from wandering into checkout, not a security control, and it is not used to protect anything sensitive.

Can I use this in a classroom?

Please do. Nothing needs installing and no child needs an account. If you want it on a school network, allowlisting the site's own domain and fonts.googleapis.com is enough.

How do I delete what's been saved?

Use Erase saved scores above, or clear site data in your browser. There is nothing stored on a server to delete.
